Value Flow Labs

Value Flow Labs builds quiet, durable tools for financial markets.

We prefer evidence to forecasts, and honest ranges to confident price targets.

Our work is made slowly, so that it can be relied upon for decades.

How we work

Three principles, kept plainly

  1. I

    Patience

    We take the long view. A tool worth building is worth building once, properly, and keeping in service.

  2. II

    Evidence

    Every method is tested against a naive baseline on real history before we rely on it or ask anyone else to.

  3. III

    Integrity

    We say what we know, what we do not, and how we found out. Honesty comes before persuasion.

The founder

Tomasz Waszczyk

Founder of Value Flow Labs. Software architect, based in Kraków.

Tomasz came to digital assets from inside finance. He holds a master’s degree in computer science, worked at an investment fund, and went on to design and build distributed and Internet-of-Things systems in Java and TypeScript as a software architect. He has published his work in the open since 2012.

He believes that hard assets, and Bitcoin first among them, will become part of the ordinary machinery of credit. Value Flow Labs exists to build that machinery carefully, and he means the company to leave its mark on how value moves in the decades ahead.

He reads history every day and keeps a public notebook of it, several hundred pages long. Financial systems are shaped by events as much as by engineers, and he would rather know how the last ones ended.
