Privacy Policy

This website is a set of plain pages. It was made to tell you about Value Flow Labs, and not to learn about you.

What this site collects

Nothing. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising, and has no forms or accounts.

Hosting

The pages are served by GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may record technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, for security and operation of the service. Value Flow Labs does not receive or use those records. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you write to us

When you send an email or a message on LinkedIn, your name, address and the content of your message are used only to read and answer it. They are not sold or passed to anyone else. You may ask at any time for your correspondence to be deleted.

Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, among them GitHub, LinkedIn, Vercel, Netlify and Videopoint. Each follows its own privacy rules once you leave these pages.

Who is responsible
